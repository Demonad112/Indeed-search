# Voice sample

Writing in Addison's own words, for Phase 3 to match. This is a real cover
letter he wrote — kept verbatim, typos and all, because the point is the voice,
not a polished specimen.

---

## Sample 1 — cover letter, retail loss prevention (verbatim)

> Thank you for your time and consideration of my application. My goals are
> quite simple, excel in my field of Loss Prevention and gain as much knowledge
> about the business I am entrusted with. In changing careers, I have a lot to
> offer, such as computer proficiency, leaderships skills, and the ability to
> collaborate with a team or personally, but I do know there is also a lot to
> learn not only about T.J. Maxx and this is where I strive best. I am adaptable
> and follow direction clearly so although my position inside your company may
> be at the starting point, this is not where I plan to stay, instead my drive is
> pointed to moving up through hard work and dedication to show the true
> protentional of my skillsets available. References, more work experience or
> anything you need is available upon your request.

---

## What to carry over

- Plain, direct sentences. States the goal early and without ceremony.
- Comfortable naming what he does not know yet and framing it as why he wants the role.
- Emphasis on progression: starting point, not the destination.
- Closes by offering references and more detail rather than over-explaining up front.
- No corporate filler, no "I am writing to express my interest", no
  "I believe I would be a great fit for your esteemed organisation".

## What to fix

The sample is unedited. Drafts should keep the voice but tighten it:

- Fix the typos (`protentional` → potential, `leaderships` → leadership).
- Shorter sentences. The original runs three clauses past where it should stop.
- Lead with something concrete from the posting instead of a thank-you.
- Name specific, verifiable experience — arrests, court disclosure, internal
  investigations, the 30-person team — rather than adjectives like "adaptable".
- Do not oversell the career change. State the two tracks (loss prevention +
  IT/network) as a combination that fits investigative work, and move on.

## Anti-patterns — never produce these

- "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- "I believe I would be an excellent fit..."
- "Your esteemed organisation" / "this exciting opportunity"
- Any claim of a certification, licence, degree or number not in resume_master.md.
